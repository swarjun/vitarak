Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Open Vitarak, pick a shop near your locality, add items to your cart, choose a saved delivery address (or add one), choose when you want it delivered, and place the order. Payment is Cash on Delivery.

How is my order delivered?

The shop prepares your order and its own delivery person brings it to you. You can follow the status in the Orders tab, and you’ll see a message when it’s on the way.

Why do some items show an estimated price?

Items sold by weight (e.g. fresh chicken or fish) are priced after weighing. You’ll see an estimate at order time and the final price once the shop weighs the item; you can decline at delivery if you don’t agree.

Can I set up recurring deliveries?

Yes — tap Subscribe on an item to receive it daily, weekly, or monthly. Manage or pause subscriptions from Account → My subscriptions.

How do I cancel an order or get a refund?

You can cancel before the order is dispatched where the app allows. Since orders are Cash on Delivery, any refund is handled directly with the shop.

I run a shop — how do I join?

Create an account as a Shop, add your shop name, category, address, and service areas, then list your items. Contact us at [support email] if you need help onboarding.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Account → Language to switch between English, मराठी, and हिंदी.
