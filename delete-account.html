Delete your account and data

Vitarak · operated by Swarjun Tech

You can ask us to delete your Vitarak account and the personal data associated with it. This page explains how to request deletion, what is deleted, and what we may keep.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

You can delete your account directly from within Vitarak:

  1. Open the Vitarak app and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm and enter your password. Your account and personal data are deleted right away, and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can’t access the app, email us instead:

  1. Send an email to swarjuntech@outlook.com from the email address registered on your Vitarak account, so we can verify you.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Vitarak account”.
  3. We will verify the request is really from you and confirm by email once it’s done.

What gets deleted

When your deletion request is completed, we remove your personal data, including:

  • Your profile (name, phone number, email) and sign-in credentials;
  • Your saved delivery addresses, map pins, and favourite shops;
  • Your subscriptions and scheduled deliveries;
  • For shop accounts: your shop listing, item catalogue, and service-area settings.

What we may keep, and why

We may retain a limited set of records where the law requires it or for legitimate business needs, for example:

  • Order and transaction records needed for tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, or legal-compliance purposes, retained for as long as the law requires. Where possible these are de-identified so they are no longer linked to you.
  • Aggregated or anonymised data that cannot identify you.

How long it takes

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — you would need to create a new account to use Vitarak again.
